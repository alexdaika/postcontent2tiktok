Privacy Policy — Postcontent2tiktok

Last updated: 10/08/2026

This app ("Postcontent2tiktok") is a personal tool used to upload video content to the developer's own TikTok account via TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What data we access

  • TikTok access token and refresh token, used only to authenticate API requests to publish video content.
  • Basic creator info (nickname, privacy settings) returned by TikTok's Creator Info API, used only to configure the post correctly.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or share data belonging to any other TikTok user.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use collected data for advertising.

Data storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the developer's own device/server and are never transmitted anywhere other than TikTok's official API endpoints.
